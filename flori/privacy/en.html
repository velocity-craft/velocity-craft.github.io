Flori Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-10

Flori (hereinafter "the App") values your personal information and has established the following privacy policy in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

1. Information Collected

Your library, quotes, photos, and notes are stored on your device by default. The App may collect the following information:

  • Book metadata queries: ISBN, title, or author terms you search are sent to public book metadata providers (Google Books, Kakao Books) to fetch cover images and bibliographic information. These queries are not associated with your identity.
  • Photos and images: Photos you capture or pick to attach to a quote are processed and stored locally on your device.
  • Account information (optional): If you sign in to enable cloud sync, your Google account ID is used to provision a sync identity through AWS Cognito. Your library, quotes, and book entries are then encrypted in transit and stored in our cloud database to mirror across your devices.
  • Premium purchase status: For premium features, the App reads premium (Lifetime) status from Google Play Billing.
  • Crash and usage diagnostics: Anonymized crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and product analytics events (Firebase Analytics) are collected to improve stability and inform feature decisions.
  • Location (optional): When you insert the location template variable into a quote or note, the App reads your last known location to fill in the value. Location data is not stored beyond the inserted text.

2. Purpose of Collection and Use

  • Book metadata: To pre-fill book information when adding a book to your library.
  • Cloud sync: To synchronize your library across multiple devices for users with a premium purchase.
  • Premium purchase: To unlock premium features.
  • Diagnostics: To detect crashes and improve service quality.
  • Location template: To insert your current location only when you explicitly use the location template variable.

3. Retention Period

  • On-device data: Deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • Cloud-synced data: Retained while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time via the contact email below.
  • Diagnostics: Subject to Google's Firebase data retention policy.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

The App does not sell your personal information. Limited data is processed by the following third parties to provide the service:

  • Google (Firebase Crashlytics, Analytics, Remote Config; Google Play Billing; Google Books API): Subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • Amazon Web Services (Cognito, DynamoDB, Lambda): Used for cloud sync of your library, only if you opt in. Subject to AWS's privacy policy.
  • Kakao Corp (Kakao Books API): Used for Korean-language book metadata lookup. Subject to Kakao's privacy policy.
  • Dropbox (optional): When you connect a Dropbox folder for backup, your Dropbox account ID and OAuth token are stored on-device. Subject to Dropbox's privacy policy.

5. App Permissions

  • Internet (INTERNET): Required for book metadata, cloud sync, purchase verification, and diagnostics.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): To detect connectivity for sync.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): For voice-to-text quote entry. [Optional]
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): For recall reminders and the daily quote. [Optional]
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC): For background sync. [Required when using cloud sync]
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): To re-register reminder notifications.
  • Exact alarm (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): For precisely-timed recall reminders. [Optional]
  • Wake lock (WAKE_LOCK): To complete background sync without interruption.
  • Coarse / Fine location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Only used when you insert the location template variable. [Optional]

Optional permissions are requested only when the corresponding feature is used.

6. Security Measures

  • All network communication uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • Authentication tokens are stored using Android's encrypted shared preferences.
  • Cloud data is transmitted with TLS and stored on managed AWS infrastructure.
