Misbah Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

Misbah (hereinafter "the App") is a Muslim prayer-times companion that respects your personal information. We have established the following privacy policy in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Misbah is designed to be privacy-first: your prayer data and location stay on your device.

1. Personal Information Collected

The App may collect the following information to provide its services.

  • Location: With your permission, approximate or precise location is used only on your device to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. Your location is never transmitted to any server. You may instead choose your city manually (offline city search), in which case no location permission is required.
  • Prayer & alarm settings: Prayer calculation method, reminders, and all alarm data (schedules, labels, sounds, settings) are stored only on your device and are not transmitted to any external server.
  • Google account email: Optionally collected when you sign in with Google to verify and restore your premium (Lifetime) purchase.
  • Advertising ID: Used by Google AdMob to display ads to users on the free tier.
  • Audio recordings: Audio captured when you use the "record alarm sound" feature is stored locally on your device only.
  • Device information: Device model and OS version may be collected for crash reporting and service improvement.

2. Purpose of Collection and Use

  • Prayer times & Qibla: Your location (or manually selected city) is used to compute daily prayer times and the direction of the Qibla on your device.
  • Reminders & alarms: Prayer and alarm data is used solely to schedule and trigger reminders and alarms on your device.
  • Premium verification: Your Google account email is used to verify and restore your premium (Lifetime) status.
  • Advertising: Displays ads through Google AdMob for users on the free tier.
  • App improvement: Anonymized crash data and usage statistics may be collected to improve service stability.

3. Retention Period

  • Location: Used transiently on-device for calculation and not stored on any server. A manually chosen city is stored only on your device.
  • Prayer & alarm data: Stored on-device only. Deleted when you remove the data or uninstall the App.
  • Audio recordings: Stored on-device only. Deleted when you remove the associated alarm sound or uninstall the App.
  • Premium data: Retained as long as necessary to verify your purchase status.
  • Advertising data: Subject to Google's privacy policy.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

The App does not share your personal information with third parties in principle. However, the following exceptions apply:

  • When you have given prior consent.
  • When required by law.
  • Limited information (advertising ID, device info) may be shared with Google AdMob for advertising services.
  • Your Google account email may be processed by our purchase-verification service (AWS) to confirm premium status.

5. Advertising and Consent

The free tier of the App displays ads via Google AdMob. For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and other applicable regions, we request consent for personalized advertising through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can review or change your advertising choices at any time in the App's settings. Removing ads is available through a premium (Lifetime) purchase.

6. App Permissions

The App uses the following permissions:

  • Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): To calculate prayer times and Qibla for your location [Optional] — manual city selection works without it
  • Schedule exact alarm (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): To fire prayer reminders and alarms at the precise time [Required]
  • Internet (INTERNET): For displaying ads and purchase verification [Required]
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): To restore your reminders and alarms automatically after a device reboot [Required]
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): For prayer-time and alarm notifications [Optional]
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): For recording custom alarm sounds [Optional]
  • Battery optimization exemption (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS): For reliable reminder delivery on devices with aggressive battery management [Optional]

Optional permissions are requested only when the corresponding feature is used. You can use the basic features of the App without granting them.

7. Data Storage and Security

The App implements the following measures to ensure the safety of your information:

  • All prayer and alarm data is stored in the device's internal storage and is not synced to any cloud service automatically.
  • Backup and restore of your data is user-initiated only.
  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) are used for all network communications.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App is intended for a general audience.
