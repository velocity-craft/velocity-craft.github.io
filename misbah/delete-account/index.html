Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: June 8, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Misbah account (the optional sign-in account used for cloud features) and the data associated with it. Misbah is published by VelocityCraft.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to velocitycraft.app+support+misbah@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in to Misbah.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my Misbah account".
  3. We will verify the request against your sign-in email and confirm by reply once the deletion is complete.

Email velocitycraft.app+support+misbah@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • Your account identifier and sign-in information (email address and the Google sign-in identifier).
  • Any data stored in the cloud and linked to your account (for example, backed-up or synced settings, when those features are in use).

What is retained, and for how long

  • Your account and associated cloud data are permanently deleted within 30 days of a verified request.
  • We do not retain account data after deletion, except where retention is required to comply with legal obligations (for example, financial or fraud-prevention records). Such records are kept only as long as the law requires and are not used for any other purpose.
  • On-device data (your alarms, prayer times, prayer records and reflections) is stored only on your device. It is removed when you delete it inside the app or uninstall Misbah, and is not affected by an account deletion request.

Signing in is optional — Misbah's core features work without an account. If you have not signed in, no account data exists to delete, and uninstalling the app removes all on-device data. Questions about your data: velocitycraft.app+support+misbah@gmail.com.
