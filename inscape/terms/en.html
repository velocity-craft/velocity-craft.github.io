4. Dropbox backup and sync

  • Backup and sync are optional and require a Dropbox account, which is governed by Dropbox's own terms. Inscape connects to Dropbox directly from your device.
  • Sync is always encrypted on your device with a sync passphrase you choose. Backup is encrypted only if you turn on backup encryption and set a passphrase.
  • A lost passphrase cannot be recovered. We never receive your passphrases, so if you forget one, the data it protects cannot be decrypted by you or by us. Keep it somewhere you will still have it after losing the phone.
  • When the same entry is changed on two devices before they sync, the App keeps both versions as separate copies rather than silently discarding one. Sync can be delayed by network conditions, battery settings, or Dropbox availability, and can be turned off temporarily from our side if a serious problem is found.
  • Files already in Dropbox stay there after you disconnect or uninstall; deleting them is up to you.
