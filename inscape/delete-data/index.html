Inscape · by Velocity Craft

Delete your Inscape data

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Inscape, the journal app published by Velocity Craft, has no user accounts and no server that holds your journal. Your entries, photos and voice memos live on your device, and in your own Dropbox only if you turned on backup or sync. You delete that data yourself; we cannot see or delete it for you.

Delete your journal data

  1. On this device: delete entries in the app, or uninstall Inscape to remove everything it stored on the device.
  2. In Dropbox (if you used backup or sync): open your Dropbox, delete the Inscape app folder, and optionally revoke Inscape in your Dropbox account settings under Connected apps. Uninstalling the app or disconnecting Dropbox does not remove files already in Dropbox.
  3. Android device backup: backups of the app in your Google account are managed in Google's backup settings.

Request deletion of analytics, crash and advertising data

Usage statistics, crash reports and advertising data are collected by Google (Firebase, AdMob). They are not linked to your name or email. To ask us to delete the Firebase data associated with your device:

  1. Email velocitycraft.app+inscape@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Inscape data".
  2. Because this data is not tied to your identity, we may not be able to locate the data from your device. We delete whatever we can identify; anything we cannot identify is removed automatically under the retention periods below.
  3. We reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Email velocitycraft.app+inscape@gmail.com

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Firebase deletes crash reports after 90 days. Event-level usage data is kept for no more than 14 months.
  • Advertising data is handled by Google under Google's own retention rules.
  • We keep nothing else about you.

Details are in the Inscape Privacy Policy.
