Inscape for Mac — Help and Support

For Inscape on macOS 14 and later

Inscape and its optional Dropbox sync are free. Dropbox requires your own account and available storage. You can write and read locally without connecting Dropbox.

Start writing

Unlock Inscape using Touch ID or your Mac login password. Press ⌘N to create an entry and start writing. The editor shows whether changes are saved. Use ⌘F to search, or browse entries from the calendar and photo library.

Moving from the direct-download Mac app

The Mac App Store edition uses a separate library. In your previous Inscape app, open Settings → Backups & restore and create an encrypted backup. In the App Store edition, choose the backup, enter its original passphrase, review the contents, and select “Add to my library.” Keep the previous app and backup until you have checked your entries and photos.

Alternatively, connect the same Dropbox account in Settings → Sync and use the existing sync passphrase. Choose existing-passphrase unlock when your journals are already in Dropbox. First-time setup is only for an empty cloud space; it does not reset an existing passphrase.

Connect two Macs

  1. On the first Mac, open Settings → Sync and connect Dropbox.
  2. For a new empty cloud space, select first-time setup, choose a passphrase of at least 12 characters, and keep a safe copy.
  3. Separately confirm creation of the encrypted cloud space to upload your library.
  4. On the other Mac, connect the same Dropbox account and unlock using the same sync passphrase. Sync to bring the entries across.

Automatic sync checks while the app is active and unlocked, and may defer while you are typing. Use “Sync now” when you want to check for changes. This Mac sync does not connect iOS or Android editions.

Sync size limits

The current sync format supports up to 64 MiB per attached file and 96 MiB per entry packet, including encoded attachments and metadata. A sync round has a 256 MiB cumulative working-data limit; encoding, repeated content, and retries count toward it. Large photo libraries can therefore exceed the limit even when individual files fit. Inscape stops with an explanation instead of silently skipping files. A streaming format for larger libraries is not available in this version.

If a sync reports a connection or size error, preserve your local library and backups. Changes already downloaded may remain after a later failure. Do not delete your library or reset its keys to retry. Contact support with the displayed message and app version.

Back up and restore

Settings → Privacy & encryption controls encryption for new backups. Settings → Backups & restore creates a backup in a location you select. Encrypted backups require their original backup passphrase. Restore previews the archive before you confirm; matching entries are reused and differing versions are preserved as separate copies.

Backup passphrases and sync passphrases are separate from your Mac login password. We cannot recover forgotten passphrases. Removing a saved passphrase does not change old backups. Deliberately exported Markdown or decrypted attachment copies are not encrypted by Inscape.

Dropbox connection and deletion

Disconnecting removes this Mac’s saved Dropbox credentials and sync passphrase, but keeps local entries and remote files. You can revoke app authorization and manage the Inscape app folder in Dropbox separately. Old encrypted objects and existing backups may remain after an entry is deleted.

Contact us

Include your Inscape version, macOS version, the action you were taking, and the error message. A screenshot with personal text hidden can help. Never send passwords, recovery keys, or private journal content.
