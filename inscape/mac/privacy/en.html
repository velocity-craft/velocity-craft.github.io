Inscape for Mac Privacy Policy

Applies to Inscape for Mac · Updated October 26, 2026

Inscape is a personal journal developed by VelocityCraft. This policy describes the Mac app. Other Inscape platforms may handle data differently.

Your journal on your Mac

You can write and read without an Inscape account or an internet connection. Journal entries, search indexes, and attachments are stored in an encrypted local library. The local encryption key is kept in this Mac’s Keychain. Display preferences such as theme and list style are stored locally as app settings.

Inscape asks macOS to authenticate you with Touch ID or your Mac login password. It receives the authentication result, not your fingerprint or Mac password. The app locks when your Mac sleeps or its user session becomes inactive, and after Inscape has been in the background for five minutes.

Encryption and the app lock do not replace your Mac’s security or protect against every process authorized to access an unlocked session. Previously exported files and operating-system backups are outside the app’s encryption controls.

Optional Dropbox sync

Sync is optional. If you connect Dropbox, authorization happens with Dropbox. Inscape stores your Dropbox account identifier, display name, and access and refresh tokens in this Mac’s Keychain to show the connected account and keep the connection working.

The app communicates directly with Dropbox and uses the app’s dedicated Dropbox folder. Journal and attachment content is encrypted on your Mac before upload and decrypted on a connected Mac using your sync passphrase. The sync passphrase is not sent to VelocityCraft or Dropbox. VelocityCraft does not operate a server that receives your journal or its encryption keys.

Dropbox stores the encrypted sync objects under your account and can observe service metadata, including object paths, sizes, revisions, request times, and your network address. Object names include hashes. This metadata is not covered by journal-content encryption. Dropbox’s account and service handling is governed by Dropbox’s privacy policy.

Sync may run while Inscape is open, active, connected, and unlocked if automatic sync is enabled. It is limited to compatible Mac libraries in this version.

Backups and exports

New backups are encrypted by default. Backup encryption uses a separate passphrase. You may choose to remember it in this Mac’s Keychain. Entry and attachment contents in an encrypted backup are protected; archive structure, file sizes, creation time, and encryption metadata remain visible.

You can deliberately turn backup encryption off or export Markdown and decrypted attachment copies. Those files can be read by someone who has access to them. You choose their destination with macOS file dialogs. If you save a file in a folder synced by another service, that service’s handling applies.

We cannot reset a forgotten sync or backup passphrase or recover an encryption key that is no longer available. Keep a recoverable backup and a safe copy of your passphrases.

Analytics, advertising, and permissions

The Mac app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or an automatic crash-report upload service. It does not use your journal for advertising. Apple may separately handle App Store, system, and optional diagnostic information under Apple’s policies.

The app reads files you select for import or restore and writes files to destinations you select for backup or export. This version imports existing photos, audio, video, and PDF files; it does not record microphone audio or collect your location, contacts, or calendar.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit, move to Trash, restore, or permanently delete entries in the app. Disconnecting Dropbox removes this Mac’s saved Dropbox credentials and sync passphrase. It does not delete your local library, erase files already stored in Dropbox, or revoke authorization in Dropbox’s account settings.

To stop Dropbox access, disconnect in Inscape and, if desired, revoke the app in your Dropbox account settings. To remove stored cloud files, manage Inscape’s app folder in Dropbox. Sync can retain older encrypted objects and deletion records; deleting an entry does not promise immediate removal of every earlier cloud object or backup copy. Backups and exported files must be managed separately wherever you saved them.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords, encryption keys, or private journal content. Contact us if you need help with a privacy request concerning support correspondence.
