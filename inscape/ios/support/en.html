Inscape for iPhone and iPad — Help and Support

For iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 and later

Write a journal, add photos, and browse your entries on iPhone and iPad. You can use your local encrypted library without connecting a cloud account.

Start writing

Unlock the app with the authentication offered by your device. Use the compose button to create an entry, add text or selected photos, and check the saved status. Search the timeline or choose a journal to narrow the list. Settings contains the app lock, backups, and sync controls.

Connect Dropbox

Open Settings → Sync and connect your Dropbox account. If you already use a compatible Inscape library with Dropbox, choose the existing-passphrase option and enter the same sync passphrase. First-time setup is for an empty cloud space; it does not reset an existing passphrase. Dropbox requires your own account and available storage.

Keep Inscape active and unlocked while syncing. If you see a connection, passphrase, or size error, preserve your library and backups and send us the displayed message. Do not delete your library or reset its keys to retry. Disconnecting keeps local entries and remote files; Dropbox authorization and stored files can be managed separately.

Back up before changing devices

The local encrypted library is excluded from ordinary operating-system backup. In Settings → Backups & Restore, enter a backup passphrase of at least 12 characters and confirm it when creating a new backup. Save the encrypted archive somewhere you can access after replacing your device.

To restore, choose the backup file and enter its original passphrase. Inspect the preview before confirming. Keep the old device and backup until you have checked your entries and photos. Backup and sync passphrases are separate from your device passcode, and we cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

Contact us

Include your Inscape version, iOS or iPadOS version, device model, the action you were taking, and the exact error. For TestFlight problems, include the build number and installation message. Hide personal text in screenshots. Never send passwords, encryption keys, or private diary content.
