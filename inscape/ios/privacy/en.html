Inscape for iPhone and iPad — Privacy Policy

Applies to the iOS and iPadOS app · Updated October 31, 2026

Inscape is a personal journal developed by VelocityCraft. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app.

Your journal on your device

You can write and read without an Inscape account or an internet connection. Entries and search data are stored in an encrypted database, and imported attachments are encrypted in the local library. The local encryption key is kept in this device’s Keychain. Display and lock preferences are stored locally.

The app can ask Apple’s system authentication to unlock using Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. It receives the result, not biometric data or your passcode. When the app is inactive it covers its interface, and when it enters the background it locks if the app lock is enabled. These protections do not prevent every screenshot or replace your device’s security.

The encrypted library is excluded from ordinary operating-system backup. Create a separate encrypted backup in the app and keep its passphrase safe before changing or erasing your device.

Optional Dropbox sync

If you connect Dropbox, authorization happens directly with Dropbox. Inscape keeps connection credentials and the sync passphrase in this device’s Keychain. It encrypts journal and attachment content before upload and decrypts it on compatible connected Inscape installations using the same sync passphrase. The passphrase is not sent to VelocityCraft or Dropbox. VelocityCraft does not operate a server receiving your journal or encryption keys.

Dropbox stores the encrypted objects in your account and receives service information including paths, sizes, revisions, request times, and your network address. This service information is outside journal-content encryption. See Dropbox’s privacy policy.

Backups, photos, and files

The iOS backup screen creates an encrypted archive with a separate passphrase and lets you choose where to share or save it. Archive structure, sizes, creation times, and encryption metadata can remain visible. A storage service you choose handles the saved archive under its own policies. We cannot recover a lost encryption key or forgotten sync or backup passphrase.

The app reads photos and backup files you choose through system pickers. This version does not record microphone audio or collect your location, contacts, or calendar. Files exported by another Inscape edition may have different protection.

Analytics and advertising

This iOS app has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or automatic crash-report upload service. Your journal is not used for advertising. Apple may separately process App Store, system, and optional diagnostic information under Apple’s policies.

Your choices and support

You can edit entries, move them to Trash, or restore them. Disconnecting Dropbox removes this device’s saved Dropbox credentials and sync passphrase, but does not delete your local library or remote files. You can revoke authorization and manage remote copies in Dropbox separately. Older encrypted objects, deletion records, and previously saved backups may remain; deleting an entry does not erase every historical copy.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments and use them to respond. Contact us about deleting support correspondence. Do not send passwords, encryption keys, or private journal content.
