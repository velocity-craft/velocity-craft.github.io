Draftly for iPhone and iPad — Help and Support

For iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 and later

Draftly brings Markdown notes, images, documents, and reminders together on iPhone and iPad. Downloading the app is free. Any purchase offered in the app shows its terms and price before you confirm with Apple.

Write and organize

Create a note from the main screen, then organize your notes with folders and tags. Templates and snippets help with repeated writing. Use search to find saved notes. You can use the local library without signing in.

Import and back up

Choose an image or document from the app’s import controls. Imported documents are copied into Draftly; editing the imported copy does not update the original file. Open Settings → Backup & Restore for available backup and import options. Keep the original library and a backup until you have checked a restore.

Dropbox sync is currently limited to eligible test accounts and requires a Dropbox account with available storage. Ordinary library content is not end-to-end encrypted by Draftly, and Vault content is excluded from the current iOS sync flow. Disconnecting does not delete remote copies. Never delete your library or reset keys just to retry a sync error.

Voice input and permissions

Voice input needs microphone and speech-recognition permission. Allow them in system Settings if you want to use this feature. Some languages or devices may require Apple’s online recognition service. Local notes remain available without this permission.

Purchases and TestFlight

If a purchase option cannot load, check your internet connection and Apple Account, then try again after the displayed loading state finishes. If Apple shows a pending approval, allow that approval to finish instead of making repeated purchases. Contact us with the app version and error message; do not send payment-card details.

For a TestFlight installation failure, include the build number and the exact message from TestFlight. An installation error does not mean your local notes should be deleted.

Contact us

Send your Draftly version, iOS or iPadOS version, device model, steps taken, and error message. Remove private text from screenshots. Do not include passwords, Vault secrets, or private notes. In-app feedback opens an email draft that you can review before sending.
