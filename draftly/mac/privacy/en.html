Draftly for Mac — Privacy Policy

Applies to the native macOS app · Updated October 31, 2026

Draftly is developed by VelocityCraft. This policy describes the Mac app. Data handling and available features can differ on iPhone, iPad, and Android.

Your notes and files on your Mac

You can write notes without signing in. Notes, imported images and documents, organization information, and settings are stored locally. The app uses the macOS sandbox and accesses files you select for import, export, or backup. Imported files are stored as app-managed copies; editing a note does not edit the original file.

Ordinary notes are not separately encrypted by Draftly. macOS account security, FileVault if enabled, and the protection of your backup locations are separate from Draftly's app lock.

Vault, app lock, and backups

Vault uses a separate local note library and an authentication gate. It does not add separate Draftly encryption to the local database or attachment files. When you use system authentication, Apple handles it and the app receives the result, not your fingerprint or Mac login password. If you set a Draftly app passcode, its verification data is kept locally in Keychain.

If you set a Vault backup passphrase, the Vault portion of a full backup is encrypted. This does not encrypt the ordinary library portion or every exported file. Without a Vault backup passphrase, a full backup can contain unencrypted Vault records. Review the selected backup options and protect the exported file. We cannot recover a forgotten backup passphrase.

Optional Dropbox access for eligible test accounts

Dropbox synchronization is currently restricted to eligible test accounts. When you connect it, the app authorizes directly with Dropbox and stores connection credentials in Keychain. Synchronization sends notes, attachments, and synchronization information to your Dropbox account. Ordinary library content does not receive end-to-end encryption from Draftly. Vault content is excluded from the current synchronization path.

Image metadata may include precise GPS coordinates already present in a selected photo. Synchronization information also includes a persistent identifier for this app installation and PDF reading positions so another device can resume at the same page. These are used for app functionality, not advertising or tracking across other apps.

Dropbox also receives normal service information such as the network address, request time, and file paths, sizes, and revisions. See the Dropbox Privacy Policy. VelocityCraft does not operate a server that receives your synchronized notes. Disconnecting stops the app's connection; it does not erase copies already stored in Dropbox or elsewhere. Manage remote files and revoke access separately in Dropbox.

Links and optional system services

Link previews may request a pasted web address and related preview resources. The websites receive those requests and normal connection information. The app also requests static configuration from velocitycraft.app and update information from Apple. It does not add note content or account identifiers to those configuration and update requests, but the services can receive an IP address and technical request information.

Where voice input is available, it requires microphone and speech recognition permission. The app requests on-device recognition when supported; otherwise, Apple's speech service may process audio remotely under your system permissions and Apple's policies. Where a template's location variable is available, it uses location permission and Apple's location and geocoding services to insert a place name. Apple may process the location to resolve that name, and the inserted text becomes part of the note. Availability depends on the Mac version and system support. You can manage permissions in System Settings.

Optional Sign in with Apple provides an identifier and any name or email address you authorize. The app keeps the local profile and credentials in Keychain for access checks; it does not send them to a VelocityCraft account server. Purchases, when offered, are handled by Apple's StoreKit. The app checks verified purchase status on the device and does not receive payment card information. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics, notifications, and sharing

The Mac app does not include an advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or automatic crash-report upload service. Optional activity logging stays on the device. Apple may separately process App Store and system diagnostics.

Notifications are used for reminders you configure. Images and documents are accessed through your selected import or file access. The current Mac app uses photo selection rather than camera capture. Selected images can contain embedded metadata. Sharing and backup create files in a location you choose. Recipients and storage providers may be able to read their contents depending on the file format and protection you select.

Your choices and support

Delete unwanted content in the app and manage backups, exported files, and cloud copies separately. Signing out removes the saved local profile; you can also manage authorization in your Apple account settings. Keep a backup you can restore before removing the app or replacing a library.

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. The in-app feedback flow prepares a message with technical details such as the app version, macOS version, memory, and language. You can review and remove those details before sending. We use the message to respond to your request. Contact us below to request deletion of support correspondence. Please do not send passwords, encryption keys, or private notes.
