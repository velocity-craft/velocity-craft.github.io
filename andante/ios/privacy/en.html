Andante for iPhone and iPad — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 3, 2026

This policy describes VelocityCraft's Andante for iPhone and iPad. Android and the separate Mac editions have their own policies. You can manage local tasks without creating a cloud account. Optional cloud synchronization is currently available only to eligible developer/test accounts; it does not start just because you install the app.

Your local library

The app stores the tasks, notes, lists, routines, habits, checklists, reading records and preferences you create or import on your device. Selected cover images and imported files are kept for the functions you choose. Andante does not add application-level encryption to the local task database or exported backup and CSV files. Device security and operating-system protection are separate. Anyone with access to an exported file may be able to read it, so choose its storage location carefully.

Optional cloud synchronization

If you have access to cloud synchronization and choose to sign in and enable it, Andante uses Amazon Cognito for account authentication and Amazon S3 for synchronized content. These services are configured in Mumbai, India (AWS ap-south-1). You choose Google or Apple as the sign-in provider; that provider also handles your sign-in under its own privacy terms.

For this feature, we process your email address, account identifiers, a randomly generated installation identifier, and the task-library content and preferences that synchronize. The installation identifier helps distinguish edits from different devices and is not an advertising identifier. Data is associated with your cloud account and used to authenticate access and synchronize your library. Images and device calendar events are not uploaded by this synchronization feature.

Cloud transfer uses HTTPS and the storage service applies server-side encryption at rest. This feature does not provide end-to-end encryption. Do not use the cloud service for information you do not want stored in this way. The local email check used to enable developer features is performed on the device and does not itself send that entered address to our servers.

Calendar, voice, widgets and sharing

With permission, calendar events can appear beside your tasks; the app reads those device events locally. Your calendar provider may separately synchronize its calendar under its own settings.

When you choose voice capture and grant access, Andante uses Apple's on-device speech recognition to turn microphone input into text. There is no server-recognition fallback. Recognition availability depends on your device, language and installed support. Text you save becomes part of your task library and can synchronize if you have enabled cloud sync.

The widget and Share extension use the app's shared local container to display or capture content. They do not independently upload it. Content later saved in the app follows the cloud-sync choice described above. Choosing a cloud drive or another app for an export gives that service a copy under its own privacy policy.

Notifications, analytics and support

Reminders may display task information in system notifications. Lock-screen previews, Focus, operating-system backups and Apple diagnostics follow your device settings. This edition has no advertising, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or developer analytics service, and does not use your information for cross-app advertising tracking.

If you email support, we receive the address, message and attachments you send. A feedback composer may prepare the app/OS version, device model and language; your mail app sends these only when you choose to send the message. Share only information needed for support.

Managing and deleting information

You can edit or delete local records in the app. Trash follows the retention setting or your permanent-delete action. Manage exported copies and operating-system backups separately.

Turning off synchronization or signing out does not delete previously synchronized cloud content. When the cloud-account controls are available, use “Delete cloud data and account” to request deletion. The app removes the current cloud state and its account; old cloud object versions are scheduled to expire after 30 days. Network failures can prevent completion and the app reports that the operation needs another attempt. If you no longer have cloud access, contact support for help deleting remaining cloud data. Cloud deletion does not remove the local library from your devices.

For privacy or support questions, contact velocitycraft.app+support+andante@gmail.com. We will update this policy when the app's data handling changes.
