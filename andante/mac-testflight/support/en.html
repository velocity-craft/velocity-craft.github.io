Andante Mac TestFlight — Help and Support

For the separate native Mac TestFlight edition, 0.1.0 (1), macOS 14 and later.

Start with your tasks

Add a task, choose a date or priority, and organize related work into lists and folders. Use Today, Next 7 Days and Search to find what needs attention. Local task management does not require an Andante account.

Your separate Mac library

This TestFlight edition does not automatically read or migrate the other Mac app’s data. It has no cloud sync, widgets or Share extension. Keep the original app and its backups if you need those records. Do not delete an existing app or library to troubleshoot TestFlight.

Optional GTD, Routines, Checklists and Habits sections can be managed through the app’s feature settings. This does not enable cloud sync in this edition.

Calendar and reminders

Allow calendar access if you want existing device events to appear alongside your tasks. You can keep managing tasks without that permission. For reminders, check the app’s notification permission and your device’s Focus and notification settings; notification delivery is controlled by the operating system.

Back up before restoring

In Settings, export a backup to a location you can access later. A backup or CSV is not encrypted; store it privately. Restoring a backup can replace the current local library. Keep the original backup and existing data until you have checked the result. A future-version backup may need a newer app or a compatibility confirmation; read the displayed message before continuing.

Installation or TestFlight problems

Send the exact error, app version/build, device model and operating-system version. Do not delete existing app data as an installation workaround. An expired or unavailable TestFlight build requires an available test build; changing your local tasks does not renew it.

Contact support

Tell us what you were doing and what happened. Remove private task text from screenshots and do not attach your full backup unless you have deliberately chosen to share its contents.
