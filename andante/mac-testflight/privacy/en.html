Andante Mac TestFlight — Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

This policy covers VelocityCraft’s native Andante Mac TestFlight edition, version 0.1.0 (1). It does not describe the separately distributed Developer ID/Sparkle Mac app or the Android app.

This edition keeps a separate sandbox-local library. It does not access or automatically migrate the other Mac edition’s library, and its cloud-sync service is disabled. Importing a backup is an explicit choice.

Local storage and protection

The app stores the content you create or import on your device: tasks, notes, dates, lists, settings and any images you choose to attach, together with records needed for the features you use. These records are kept in the app’s local storage.

Andante does not add its own encryption to the local task database or exported backup and CSV files. The operating system’s access controls and device protection are separate. Someone with access to an exported file can read its contents; choose its location carefully.

Calendar, photos and files

If you grant calendar access, the app can display events from your device calendar alongside tasks. This edition reads those events; it does not upload them to us or include them in its task backup. Calendar accounts may synchronize through Apple or another calendar provider under that provider’s settings.

Choosing a photo or file gives the app the selected content for that action. Exporting a backup or CSV writes the file to the location you select. If you choose a cloud drive or another app, that service handles the copy under its own terms and privacy policy.

Optional voice capture

When you choose voice capture and grant microphone and speech-recognition access, the app sends microphone buffers to Apple’s on-device speech recognizer to turn speech into task text. It requires on-device recognition and does not fall back to server recognition. The transcript you keep becomes local task content. Availability depends on the device and installed recognition support.

Notifications and system services

With permission, reminders can display task information in system notifications. Lock-screen previews follow your device settings. Operating-system backups, device transfer and system diagnostics are managed by Apple; they may include app data or technical information depending on your settings. These are separate from an Andante cloud-sync service.

Ads, analytics and cloud accounts

This edition does not include advertising, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or an Andante analytics service. It does not automatically send your task library to VelocityCraft. Local use does not require an Andante account.

Support and links you open

If you email support, we receive the address and message you send, along with any attachments or device details you choose to include. The app’s feedback composer can prepare the app version, operating-system version, device model and language; your mail app sends the message only when you choose to send it. Include only what is needed to explain the issue.

Opening a website or a mail link uses your browser or mail provider, which handles that request under its own privacy policy. Those services are separate from the app’s local task library.

Managing and deleting data

You can edit and delete records in the app. Items in Trash are permanently removed according to the app’s retention setting, or when you permanently delete them. Copies you exported and operating-system backups must be managed separately where they are stored. Keep a backup before replacing a device or restoring another library; we do not have a server copy of this edition’s local tasks to recover for you.

For questions about this policy or personal information you sent to support, contact velocitycraft.app+support+andante@gmail.com. We will update this page if the edition’s data handling changes.
