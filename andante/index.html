Core

A Due Time, and a Reminder That Means It

Most to-do apps let you pick a date and then whisper at you. In Andante you pick the minute, then choose per task: a notification, or a real alarm that rings until you turn it off and can be snoozed. Set a default style, sound, and volume once — override it on the tasks that deserve it. Alarms survive a reboot.

Straight Answers

No Account Needed. Nothing to Buy.

We’d rather tell you the actual conditions than sell you a word.

Free, with no paywall

No subscription, no pro tier, no locked feature. Andante is supported by an occasional ad when you come back to the app.

Your tasks stay on this device

They live in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox — not encrypted, and we won’t claim otherwise. They reach us only if you sign in to optional Andante Cloud sync.

What the app sends

Crash reports and usage counts, never with your name or email, and never your task titles, list names, or notes.

Alarms that tell the truth

Exact alarms need a permission Android grants separately. Andante asks in context, tells you when it’s missing, and never pretends a reminder is armed when it isn’t.
