Features

Everything You Need to Run the Day

Organize the list, see it beside your calendar, keep notes and goals next to the work, and take it all with you.

📥

Lists, Folders, Tags & Subtasks

Unfiled tasks wait in the Inbox. Sort them into lists, group lists into folders, add tags and priorities, and break big work into subtasks as deep as you need.

☀️

Today, Next 7 Days & Search

Start from what’s due today and the days ahead, see the next seven days or everything at once — and find any task by its words.

📅

Calendar Beside Your Meetings

Your tasks sit next to the events already on your phone’s calendar. Drag an event to a new time and Andante moves it in your calendar.

📝

Notes Next to the Work

The Notebook keeps meeting notes, ideas and plans in the same app as your tasks.

🧮

Checklists That Count

Give checklist items an amount, set a budget or goal, and see the total and what’s left.

🔁

Repeating Tasks & Projects

Repeat any task daily, weekly, monthly, yearly or on your own schedule, and complete a whole project in one step when it’s done.

⏰

Due Times & Reminders

Set a due date and time, and get reminded at the due time or up to a day before — as a notification, or an alarm for the tasks that can’t be missed.

📊

Stats & Widget

Track what you’ve completed, and keep today’s tasks on your home screen with quick add.

📦

Bring Your Tasks With You

Import from TickTick, Todoist, Google Tasks, or Apple Reminders. Back up and restore everything, or export to CSV.

🌐

Straight Answers

No Account Needed. Nothing to Buy.

We’d rather tell you the actual conditions than sell you a word.

Free, with no paywall

No subscription, no pro tier, no locked feature. Andante is supported by an occasional ad when you come back to the app.

Your tasks stay on this device

They live in the app’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox — not encrypted, and we won’t claim otherwise. They reach us only if you sign in to optional Andante Cloud sync.

What the app sends

Crash reports and usage counts, never with your name or email, and never your task titles, list names, or notes.

Reminders that tell the truth

Alarm-style reminders need a permission Android grants separately. Andante asks in context, tells you when it’s missing, and never pretends a reminder is set when it isn’t.
