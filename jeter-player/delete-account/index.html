Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: June 8, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Jeter Player account (the optional sign-in account) and its associated data. Jeter Player is published by VelocityCraft.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to velocitycraft.app+support+jeter@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in to Jeter Player.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my Jeter Player account".
  3. We will verify the request against your sign-in email and confirm by reply once deletion is complete.

Email velocitycraft.app+support+jeter@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • Your account identifier and sign-in information (email address and the Google sign-in identifier).
  • The analytics and diagnostics identifiers associated with your account.

What is retained, and for how long

  • Your account and associated identifiers are permanently deleted within 30 days of a verified request.
  • We do not retain account data after deletion, except where retention is required to comply with legal obligations. Such records are kept only as long as the law requires.
  • On-device data is stored only on your device and is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall Jeter Player.
  • If you enabled cloud backup, those files are stored in your own cloud account (e.g. your Dropbox) which you control — delete them from that account directly.

Signing in is optional — Jeter Player's core features work without an account. If you have not signed in, no account data exists to delete, and uninstalling the app removes all on-device data. Questions: velocitycraft.app+support+jeter@gmail.com.
