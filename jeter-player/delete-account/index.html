Jeter Player · by Velocity Craft

Delete your Jeter Player data

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Jeter Player, the video player published by Velocity Craft, has no user accounts — there is no account to delete — and no server that holds your videos. Your videos, Vault and library data live on your device, in your own Dropbox only if you turned on Dropbox backup, and in your Android device backup if that is on. You delete that data yourself; we cannot see or delete it for you.

Delete your videos and library data

  1. On this device: delete videos and Vault items in the app, or uninstall Jeter Player to remove the Vault and everything else it keeps in its private storage. Uninstalling permanently deletes the files in the Vault, so move out anything you want to keep first. Screenshots and screen recordings saved to your shared Pictures and Movies folders stay until you delete them.
  2. In Dropbox (if you used Dropbox backup): open your Dropbox, delete the Jeter Player folder, and optionally revoke Jeter Player in your Dropbox account settings under Connected apps. Uninstalling the app or disconnecting Dropbox does not remove files already in Dropbox.
  3. Android device backup: backups of the app in your Google account are managed in Google's backup settings.

Request deletion of analytics, crash and advertising data

Usage statistics, crash reports and advertising data are collected by Google (Firebase, AdMob). None of this is linked to your name or email. To ask us to delete the data associated with your device:

  1. Email velocitycraft.app+support+jeter@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Jeter Player data".
  2. Because this data is not tied to your identity, we may not be able to locate the data from your device. We delete whatever we can identify; analytics and crash data we cannot identify is removed automatically under the retention periods below.
  3. We reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Email velocitycraft.app+support+jeter@gmail.com

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Firebase deletes crash reports after 90 days. Event-level usage data is kept for no more than 14 months.
  • Advertising data is handled by Google under Google's own retention rules.
  • We keep nothing else about you.

Details are in the Jeter Player Privacy Policy.
