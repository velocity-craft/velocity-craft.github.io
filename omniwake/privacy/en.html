OmniWake Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This policy covers OmniWake for Android, published by Velocity Craft (“we”).

Your alarms are never sent to us

OmniWake requires no account, and Velocity Craft runs no server that stores alarms. Your alarms (times, labels, schedules, folders and tags), trips, personal events and blackout periods, the sounds you record or capture, and your alarm videos stay on your device (and in your Android device backup, if it is on; see “Where your alarm data is kept” below), and none of them is passed to the advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services described next.

There is no sign-in for users. The app contains a hidden developer panel with Google sign-in, used for internal testing. Signing in there sends nothing to us or to Google's analytics. Only when one of our own designated test accounts signs in is a one-way hash of its email address attached to analytics and crash reports from that device.

What is collected, and by whom

This is a separate matter from your alarms, and we keep the two descriptions apart on purpose. None of the following contains alarm labels, alarm times, or your recordings and videos. OmniWake shows advertising through Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase for usage analytics (Firebase Analytics), crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and remote feature settings (Firebase Remote Config). Together they collect:

CategoryCollected byWhat and why
IP addressGoogle AdMob, Google FirebaseUnavoidably included in every network request these services make
User product interactionsGoogle AdMob, Firebase AnalyticsAd impressions and clicks; which screen of the app was opened (screen names such as “settings”, never alarm content); a one-time “first alarm created” event with its alarm and schedule type; when an alarm is dismissed or snoozed, the alarm type, how it was dismissed and whether it was a FailSafe alarm; wake-up mission start, completion or timeout with the mission type and difficulty; review-prompt requests; the outcome of Google Play billing checks; failures of the app's own trip, personal-event and blackout-sync checks; and when an alarm is skipped because of one of your personal events (the event's name and date are not sent)

Purchase historyFirebase AnalyticsWhen you buy OmniWake Lifetime through Google Play, Google Analytics may record the product ID, currency and price. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your payment details

Analytics events also carry the app version, build type, your tier (Free or Lifetime) and a device performance class (low/mid/high), so that reports can be grouped.

Google Analytics also records some information automatically: device model, Android version, language setting, and session and engagement data such as first open and time in app, together with a random app-instance identifier. Firebase keeps crash reports for 90 days; analytics event data is kept for no more than 14 months.

The following are not collected and never leave your device through us: alarm labels and times, audio (your recordings and captured sounds), photos and videos (including alarm videos and screen recordings), files and documents, contacts, calendar, personal info, and precise location. Google may estimate an approximate, city-level region from the IP address for ad delivery and analytics reports; in Google Play's Data safety section this is declared as “Approximate location”. OmniWake does not request location permission and never reads your device's location.

If you buy OmniWake Lifetime, or while a Plus Pass you unlocked by watching a short ad is active, ads are not shown. The advertising SDK may still start on your device so that the consent setting and ad-free state can be checked. Analytics and crash reporting work the same on every tier.

Where your alarm data is kept

Any statement about encryption is only true under specific conditions. Here are all the places where OmniWake keeps or sends your alarm data:

PlaceEncrypted?
Alarms, settings, folders, tags, trips, personal events and blackout periods on this deviceNot by OmniWake. Kept in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Uninstalling OmniWake removes it. The optional app lock is a lock on the app, not encryption.
Sounds you record, audio you capture from your phone, alarm videos and screen recordingsNot by OmniWake. Kept in the app's private storage, or, for a video you choose, played from where it is stored on your device. A song you pick from your music library is played from the library. OmniWake never uploads any of them.
Backup file you export (omniwake_backup.gz)No. It is a compressed file containing your alarm settings and labels, schedules, folders, tags and blackout periods, and references to the sounds (not the sound or video files themselves). It goes wherever you save it, and anyone with access to that place can read it.
Android OS device backup (Auto Backup / device transfer)Not by OmniWake. If Android backup is on, your alarm database, settings, recordings and alarm videos are included in the backup to your Google account, which Google manages, and in transfers to a new device. OmniWake excludes only its purchase-state data. You can turn Android backup off in Android settings.

Features that contact other services

  • Fonts. Some of OmniWake's interface fonts, and any fonts you browse or choose in Settings, are downloaded from Google Fonts through Google Play services on your device.
  • Additional languages are downloaded on demand from Google Play when you choose a language whose files are not yet on your device.
  • App updates and reviews are offered through Google Play's in-app update and in-app review.

Third parties

  • Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform — advertising and consent. Governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase — Analytics usage events, Crashlytics crash reports, and Remote Config for feature settings. Alarm content is never passed to Firebase. Governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Play — Billing for the Lifetime purchase (we never see your payment details), in-app updates, in-app reviews, and language downloads.
  • Google Fonts — downloadable fonts.
  • Google sign-in — only in the hidden developer panel described above.

Permissions

  • Alarms — exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM), the full-screen ring view on the lock screen (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT), restoring your alarms after a restart (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED), keeping the device awake and vibrating while an alarm rings (WAKE_LOCK, VIBRATE), and a foreground service for ringing and playback (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, special use “alarm”, media playback).
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — alarm notifications and reminders.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — only while you record your own alarm sound or a screen recording for a video alarm, and only after you allow it. The recording stays on your device.
  • Music and audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO; READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and earlier) — so that you can pick a song from your library as an alarm sound.
  • Screen capture (foreground service for media projection and microphone) — only when you capture audio playing on your phone as an alarm sound, or record your screen for a video alarm. Android asks you to confirm each time, and the result stays on your device.
  • Google Play billing (BILLING) — the Lifetime purchase.
  • Internet, network state and advertising ID — added by the Google SDKs for ads, analytics, crash reports and billing. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

The microphone and screen capture are requested only when you use the feature that needs them. To make alarms reliable on devices with aggressive battery management, OmniWake can open Android's battery-optimization settings so that you can exempt it; this needs no permission. OmniWake does not request access to your location, camera, or contacts.

European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

Where required, OmniWake presents a Google-certified consent form before showing any ads. Depending on your choice, ads are shown in non-personalized form or not loaded at all. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Help & Feedback → Ad privacy choices. This form governs advertising only. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are not controlled by it and run on every device; Analytics can read the advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in Android settings.

You have the right to access, correct, and delete your data. Your alarms, recordings and videos are held only on your device and in storage you control, so you exercise those rights directly: edit or delete your alarms in the app, uninstall OmniWake to remove everything it keeps on this device (including recordings and videos), delete any backup file you exported, and manage your Android device backup in Android settings. Analytics, crash and advertising data are held by Google and are not linked to your name or email (except our own designated test accounts, described above); for that data, use Google's tools or contact us and we will help where we can. See how to delete your OmniWake data.

Children

OmniWake is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
